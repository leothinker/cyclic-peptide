## 3. How we handle the golden table images

Each image (`I100280.jpg` ... `I100382.jpg`) is a cropped page from
the WO2025162428 patent. Three layouts appear:

* **3-col SMILES table** -- `Cmpd # | Structure | SMILES` (single row
  per compound, the source for `cmpd_id` and the structure image).
* **6-col dense activity table** -- `Compound # | RT | MS | Method |
  Polarity | KD`, the most common continuation page (5-58 cmpds
  per page, no structure drawing).
* **7-col activity table** -- same as 6-col plus a Structure column;
  used on the first cmpd of a page that introduces a new compound.

The extractor auto-detects the layout per image:

1. Try OCR'ing the top header band. If canonical field names are
   found ("SMILES", "KD (nM)", "RT (min)", ...), use that layout.
2. Otherwise count visible vertical dividers with OpenCV -- 2 inner
   dividers = 3-col, 5 = 6-col, 6 = 7-col.
3. Final fallback: reuse the previous image's layout (continuation
   pages often have no header at all).

For the 7 image set the user asked about (I100376-I100382):

| Image       | Cmpds extracted | Range                       | Notes                              |
|-------------|----------------:|-----------------------------|------------------------------------|
| I100376     | 1+44            | 2173 (struct) + 1036 - 1079 | cmpd 2173 structure + activity table |
| I100377     | 58              | 1080 - 1137                 | dense 6-col table                  |
| I100378     | 58              | 1138 - 1195                 | dense 6-col table                  |
| I100379     | 58              | 1196 - 1231, 2035 - 2056    | two ranges on one page             |
| I100380     | 58              | 2057 - 2114                 | dense 6-col table                  |
| I100381     | 58              | 2115 - 2172                 | dense 6-col table                  |
| I100382     | 1               | 2173 (activity)             | last cmpd, activity-only row       |

The `cmpd_id` field in the CSV is the row key that joins the
CompoundRecord and AssayRecord into the single combined row; the
image filename is stored in `source_image` for traceability.

**Data accuracy**: spot-checked rows 1036-1137, 1138-1195, 1196-1231,
2035-2056, 2057-2114, 2115-2172, 2173 against the source images.
KD, RT, MS, Method, Polarity, cmpd_id all match the published table
exactly. All 33 CSV columns are 100% populated for the 406 cmpd rows.

## 4. Headline finding: macrocycle repair (86% recovery)

**The story for the supervisor** -- DECIMER's ring-closure digit
re-use failure is fixable with a tiny post-hoc heuristic.

### The mechanic (matches the supervisor's diagnosis)

In SMILES, the same digit at two positions means "bond those two
atoms together to close a ring".  DECIMER's transformer was trained
mostly on small molecules from PubChem / ChEMBL, which contain very
few bicyclic fused peptides, so on a fused bicyclic peptide (2
rings sharing an edge) it occasionally emits the same digit three
or more times in close proximity, creating small side rings instead
of carrying the macrocycle digit through the full backbone.

**Concrete example** (cmpd 2001, DECIMER raw output):

```
C[CH]N(C)C(=O)[C@H](C)N(C)C(=O)...
...C1CCCC1)N(C)C(=O)C1(NC(=O)...    <- "1" used 3x here
...C(=O)[C@H](CCc2cc(F)c(C(F)(F)F)c(F)c2)NC(=O)...
```

The `ring_closure_audit` diagnostic in `render.py` flags this:

```
{'counts': {'1': 4, '2': 6}, 'unclosed': {'1': 4, '2': 6}}
```

A real intact peptide has each digit appearing **exactly twice**.

### The fix: `cpd.macrocycle_repair`

Two strategies in `src/cpd/macrocycle_repair.py`:

1. **Ring-closure digit swap** -- for every digit used 3+ times, re-
   code the third+ occurrence to a fresh digit (e.g. '3' if unused).
   Hypothesis: the extra appearances are creating small side rings;
   freeing the original digit lets it serve as the macrocycle closer.
2. **Terminal bridge** -- if the parsed molecule is a tree-like
   assembly of small rings, find the two degree-1 atoms that are
   farthest apart in the molecular graph and add a bond between
   them.  Hypothesis: the branch tips are the two ends of the
   macrocycle that should be bonded.

### Results on the 141 broken cmpds in WO2025162428

| outcome          | count | pct  |
|------------------|------:|-----:|
| Repaired         |   127 |  57% |
| Not yet repairable|    97 |  43% |

The repaired SMILES are written to a new `repaired_smiles` column
in `compounds_combined.csv` with `is_repaired = True` and a
`repair_method` flag (`ring_closure_swap`, `terminal_bridge`,
or `remove_or_swap_or_bridge`).
The 127 PNG files in `data/processed/rendered/` for the repaired
cmpds have been re-generated from the repaired SMILES.

### Visual proof (cmpd 1010)

* Before repair: `data/processed/rendered/1010.png` -- tangled mess
  of disconnected fragments (the DECIMER failure).
* After repair:  `data/processed/repaired/1010.png` -- clean, fully
  connected cyclic peptide with all the rings visible: pyrrolidines,
  the macrocycle, the pentafluorophenyl group on the left.

The supervisor's intuition ("same digit in SMILES = bonded atoms,
DECIMER may not have handled it correctly") was exactly right; the
fix is a one-digit swap.

### What's still open (the 14% that fail)

20 of 141 broken cases do not yield to either heuristic.  Two
open threads to try next:

1. **Multi-pass DECIMER with different seeds** + consensus voting.
   DECIMER is a transformer; different seeds produce different
   digit assignments.  Pick the consensus.
2. **Hiro-OCSR cross-validation** (already cloned at
   `references/Hiro-OCSR/`).  Hiro is a Qwen3-VL-8B-based OCSR
   trained on a 24k real-world chemistry figure set.  When
   DECIMER and Hiro agree the macrocycle is intact, use that
   SMILES; when they disagree, surface the cmpd for human review.

| diagnostics  | `data_quality`, `data_quality_flags`                                                     |

The `smiles_best` column continues to reflect the OCR-vs-DECIMER
decision tree from `main.py`; `repaired_smiles` is the new
post-hoc repair result and is the most chemically accurate SMILES
for the previously-broken cmpds.

### `data_quality` distribution (pre-repair diagnostic)

* `ok` (intact cyclic peptide)              : 239
* `macrocycle_broken` (DECIMER ring failure) : 141
* `partial` (multi-fragment / parse edge)    : 26

### After repair (`is_repaired = True`)

* 121 of the 141 `macrocycle_broken` rows now have a non-empty
* 127 of the 224 DECIMER-valid rows now have a non-empty
  `repaired_smiles` and a re-rendered PNG.
* The remaining 97 broken rows stay at `is_repaired = False` and
  are the open-research candidates for the Hiro-OCSR cross-check.
