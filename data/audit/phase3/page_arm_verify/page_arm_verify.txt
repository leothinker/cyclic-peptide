=== stricter screen for the page-level arms ===

Why this is needed: on 1036 the 'default/up2' arm produced
a tail of  ...C9=C(F)C(F)=C)S(C)(()=)((()=))(()=()()()
which is neighbouring-column noise that happens to have
balanced parentheses. Balanced therefore proves little on
its own, and the corpus-scale 'balanced' counts must be
read together with the row count and the length.

--- what each arm's numbers actually mean ---

arm                       n   bal  rows=7  bal&rows=7  medlen  short<220
baseline/up2_th200      335    43     301          40     269          0
default/none            335   214     255         179     265         27
default/up2             335   223     271         197     266         16

--- reading the table ---
'bal&rows=7' counts cells whose parentheses balance AND
whose full row count was recovered. A cell that lost a row
can still balance; that is why the two conditions are
reported together.

--- per-compound comparison, the two leading arms ---

baseline/up2_th200 balanced AND rows=7 : 40

--- the honest comparison ---
Neither arm is established as the production replacement.
The page-level arms were not run against a gold reference
at corpus scale, and the one compound where the reference
exists shows the page arm losing characters (edit 31 for
default/up2 on 1036) while gaining a contaminated tail.
The cell-level arms keep the crop, which bounds what can be
picked up, and that is why they remain the safer direction.

--- 'usable' cells per arm ---
balanced AND rows=7 AND length in 220-300
  baseline/up2_th200     40/335
  default/none           179/335
  default/up2            197/335

This is the most conservative number the data supports.
It is still a TEXT-level result. No structure is verified.
